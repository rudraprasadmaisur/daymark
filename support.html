How can we help?

Pre-release support

Daymark is currently in development. A dedicated, monitored support contact will be added before public launch. Please do not post journal entries, verification codes or private account information publicly.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Daymark Plus and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account used to subscribe.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open You, then Privacy & account. Use the export, backup or account deletion controls. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I manage notifications?

Change your reminder in Daymark or manage notification permissions in iOS Settings.
