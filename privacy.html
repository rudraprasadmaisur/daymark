Pre-release draft · subject to verification

Your life is yours.

This draft describes the intended data handling of the current pre-release Daymark iOS build. It must be reviewed and updated before public distribution, particularly if analytics or a new email provider is enabled.

Account information

Daymark uses Supabase to process an email address and authenticate an account. Authentication session credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. The configured backend is hosted in Singapore.

Personal entries

Profile preferences, daily check-ins, intentions, habits, reflections and focus history are stored locally by account. If you choose to back up your entries, a copy is uploaded to the account's private backup in Supabase. The backup is not end-to-end encrypted. It is protected through authenticated access policies.

Subscriptions

Apple processes in-app payments and manages subscriptions. Daymark uses StoreKit to determine subscription access. Daymark does not receive your card details.

Analytics

The supplied Typography Edition does not include a third-party analytics SDK. The upcoming release candidate adds optional PostHog usage analytics; it is off by default. If you opt in, feature event names, technical device information and a pseudonymous account identifier may be processed in the US. Daymark does not intentionally send your journal text, task descriptions, individual mood responses, email address or personal goals to PostHog. You may turn usage analytics off in the app. Analytics retention and deletion handling must be verified before public distribution.

Your choices

Use the You tab to export personal entries, delete a cloud backup or delete your account. Signing out does not erase local entries. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in Apple account settings. Retention periods for service logs and deletion verification remain to be finalized before release.

Contact

See the support page. A monitored support contact must be confirmed before this draft becomes the live privacy policy.
